How it works

Up and running in under two minutes

No installers, no bundled toolbars — just the loader and the game.

1

Download the kit

Grab the latest Crossout Boost Kit archive from the download page and extract it to a folder you control. Windows 10 and 11 are both supported.

2

Launch Crossout, then run the loader

Start Crossout PC and log into the garage. Run the injector loader as administrator — it detects the game process and injects the kit automatically.

3

Toggle modules in the overlay

Press the configured hotkey to open the overlay, then enable aimbot, ESP, the part farm bot or instant reload. Settings save to your profile instantly.
